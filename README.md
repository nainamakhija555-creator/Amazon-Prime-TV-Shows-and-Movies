# 📖 Case Study – Amazon Prime TV Shows & Movies Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Amazon Prime TV Shows and Movies dataset to uncover meaningful insights into the platform's content library. Using Python and data analysis techniques, the dataset was cleaned, processed, and visualized to understand trends in content type, genres, ratings, release years, and country-wise distribution.
The goal of this project is to demonstrate a complete data analysis workflow—from data preprocessing to business insight generation—using real-world streaming platform data.

---

## Business Problem

Amazon Prime hosts thousands of movies and TV shows from different countries and genres. As the content library grows, understanding content trends becomes essential for making informed business decisions related to content acquisition, audience engagement, and platform growth.

This analysis helps answer questions such as:

* What type of content dominates the platform?
* Which countries contribute the most titles?
* Which genres are most common?
* How has content grown over the years?
* What audience ratings are most represented?

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
* Handle missing values and duplicate records.
* Explore the distribution of Movies and TV Shows.
* Identify the top content-producing countries.
* Analyze release year trends.
* Examine audience ratings and content duration.
* Identify the most popular genres.
* Generate business insights using visualizations.

* Identified and handled missing values.

* Standardized categorical values.
* Checked dataset consistency.
* Prepared clean data for visualization and analysis.

## 💡 Key Findings

* Movies significantly outnumber TV Shows.
* The United States contributes the highest number of titles.
* Drama and Comedy are the most popular genres.
* Amazon Prime's catalogue has expanded rapidly in recent years.
* Content is available across multiple audience age groups and categories.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can help improve content strategy:

* Increase investment in regional and multilingual content to reach a broader audience.
* Expand the production and acquisition of original TV series to improve viewer retention.
* Continue investing in high-performing genres such as Drama and Comedy.
* Use historical content trends to support future content acquisition decisions.
* Personalize content recommendations based on genre and audience rating preferences.
